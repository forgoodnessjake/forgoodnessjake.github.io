Privacy Policy

Church Feud for Mac and Feud Remote for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 2026

Church Feud and Feud Remote do not collect, store, or share any personal information. I, the developer, never receive anything you enter into either app.

What the apps store

Church Feud saves the questions and answers you play and write, your team names, and your settings on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded or synced. Deleting a round in the app deletes it from your Mac, and deleting the app removes the rest.

Feud Remote remembers only whether you last used it as the buzzer or as the host's remote. It keeps no copy of the game.

Local network

Both apps ask for local network access so they can find each other. Church Feud announces itself on your local network using Bonjour, and Feud Remote connects to it directly, over Wi‑Fi or a peer-to-peer Wi‑Fi link between the two devices. Nothing goes to the internet.

Over that connection, the Mac sends team names, scores, and the current question with its answers, and Feud Remote sends back buzzes and the host's commands, such as revealing an answer. None of it is saved by Feud Remote or sent anywhere else. You can turn local network access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on iPhone or iPad, or System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the Mac. Both apps still work, just not with each other.

What the apps don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No internet connections and no servers of my own; the only connection is between the two apps on your local network.
  • No third-party services or SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because Church Feud and Feud Remote collect no personal information, they collect none from children.
