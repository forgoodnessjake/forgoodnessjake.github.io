Bank

A scorekeeper for the dice game Bank.

How to play

  1. Add at least two players and choose how many rounds to play. Players take turns in the order listed.
  2. Each turn, roll two real dice and tap the total. Rolls add to a shared bank.
  3. On the first three rolls of a round, a 7 adds 70 points.
  4. From the fourth roll on, a 7 busts: the bank is lost for everyone who hasn't banked. Doubles become available and double the bank.
  5. Any player can tap Bank at any time to add the current bank to their score. They sit out the rest of the round.
  6. The round ends when everyone has banked or someone rolls a 7. The highest score after the last round wins.

FAQ

I tapped the wrong number. Tap the Undo button at the top right, or shake your iPhone.

What happens if I close the app mid-game? Your game is saved on your device and picks up where you left off.
